abstract: We show that the Chiral Mean-Field model (CMF) can produce a phase diagram
  with three critical points... the nuclear liquid-gas transition, quark deconfinement,
  and a strangeness driven transition. The strangeness driven transition separates
  a mainly nucleonic phase from one dominated by hyperons and baryon resonances. We
  discuss the compositional change in these transitions and their possible signatures
  in heavy-ion collisions and neutron star mergers.
