abstract: We predict a new viscochiral regime of electronic transport in which spatially
  varying Hall viscosity selects vortical flow patterns. Although uniform Hall viscosity
  cannot alter incompressible bulk flow, its spatial gradient redistributes vorticity,
  amplifying vortices in one chamber while suppressing the vortex in the other. We
  further determine the underlying mechanism to be generic to recirculating flows
  and insensitive to the details of the device geometry. The paper maps the resulting
  phase diagram and shows that the regime is experimentally accessible in valley-polarized
  bilayer graphene.
