abstract: Tidal Love numbers characterize the conservative response of compact objects
  to external perturbations and provide a powerful probe of their nature. While the
  static Love numbers of Schwarzschild, Kerr, and Reissner--Nordström black holes
  remarkably vanish, the tidal response of a generic Kerr--Newman black hole has remained
  unexplored due to the intrinsic coupling between gravitational and electromagnetic
  perturbations. Exploiting a recent breakthrough in the separability of these perturbations,
  we analytically determine the static tidal response of the most general stationary
  black hole in four-dimensional Einstein--Maxwell theory. For arbitrary multipole
  order, charge, and spin, we show that the entire matrix of conservative Love numbers
  vanishes, including the mixed gravito-electromagnetic couplings. We also compute
  the nonvanishing static dissipative response induced by frame dragging.
