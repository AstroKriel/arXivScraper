abstract: In collisionless plasma turbulence, energy is transferred from electromagnetic
  fluctuations to the particles. However, the relative contributions of different
  ion energisation mechanisms remain unclear. We investigate ion energisation in three-dimensional
  hybrid Vlasov--Maxwell simulations of decaying turbulence at $β_{i0}=0.25$ and $β_{i0}=4$
  , where $β_{i0}$ is the initial ratio of ion thermal to magnetic pressure. Using
  the exact ion momentum equation, we decompose the ion energisation rate $\mathbf{j}_i\mathbf{\cdot}\mathbf{E}$
  into the work done on the parallel, diamagnetic, curvature, agyrotropic and polarisation
  ion currents. This decomposition requires neither gyrotropy nor scale separation
  and, therefore, remains valid where guiding-centre theory breaks down. We show that
  the agyrotropic term, which has no guiding-centre counterpart, accounts for $21\,\%$
  of the energisation at $β_{i0}=0.25$ and $91\,\%$ at $β_{i0}=4$ . We find that the
  dominant instantaneous energisation term changes with $β_{i0}$ , from the polarisation
  current at low $β_{i0}$ to the diamagnetic and agyrotropic currents at high $β_{i0}$
  , at all resolved scales. In addition, we show that reversible exchange with the
  ion bulk flow dominates the instantaneous rate, so the time-integrated energy transfer
  is larger at $β_{i0}=4$ even though the instantaneous rate is smaller. Our results
  provide quantitative evidence that guiding-centre drifts alone cannot describe ion
  energisation in kinetic-scale turbulence and that the dominant energisation term
  depends on $β_i$ . The results also highlight the key role of non-local thermodynamic-equilibrium
  effects in energy conversion in collisionless plasma turbulence.
