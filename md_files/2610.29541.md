abstract: Axion-like particles are light pseudoscalars that appear in many extensions
  of the Standard Model and are candidates for dark matter. Through their mixing with
  photons, they imprint energy dependent modulations on the spectra of astrophysical
  sources shining through magnetised media. When a distant source shines through the
  turbulent magnetic field of an intervening galaxy cluster, these modulations are
  stochastic. It has recently been shown that averaging over an ensemble of such sources
  instead produces a smooth absorption-like feature. In this work we derive the analytic
  form of this ensemble averaged signal, previously known only numerically. We further
  relate the index $a$ of the magnetic field energy spectrum to the index $β$ of the
  domain size distribution that simulations use to describe the turbulent field. For
  $1<a<2$ the two indices obey the simple analytic relation $β=3-a$ . Any distribution
  with $β<1$ instead mimics the spectral index $a=2$ , which the sharp domain walls
  impose. For Kolmogorov turbulence, relevant for galaxy clusters, the relation gives
  $β=4/3$ .
