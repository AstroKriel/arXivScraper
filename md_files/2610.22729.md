abstract: Nonlinear interactions in incompressible magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) and Navier-Stokes
  turbulence involve three spatial fluctuations conservatively exchanging, e.g., energy.
  The mathematical structure of a triad interaction does not allow to unambigously
  determine the specific contribution of each of the participating fluctuations to
  the resulting exchanges. Presently, only additional assumptions allow for the deconstruction
  of triad interactions into a sum of pair exchanges. This work proves that in incompressible
  MHD the simultaneous nonlinear transfer of cross-helicity sufficiently constrains
  the triad dynamics such that a mathematically unique pair-exchange representation
  of nonlinear energy and cross-helicity transfer is possible.
