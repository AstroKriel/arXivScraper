abstract: The macroscopic ideal-MHD stability of an axisymmetric mirror device with
  sonic levels of plasma rotation is analyzed by approximating the plasma equilibrium
  as a rotating theta pinch possessing an artificial gravity. An eigenmode equation
  is derived that governs the stability of the equilibrium to small perturbations
  in the case of an arbitrary plasma angular velocity profile. The stability of the
  m=1 and m=2 modes is investigated. The plasma is found to be stable to these two
  modes provided that it is sufficiently short in the axial direction. The critical
  axial length of the device below which the modes are stabilized first decreases
  with increasing plasma rotation, attains a minimum value when the rotation is roughly
  sonic, and then increases with increasing plasma rotation. The value of the plasma
  rotation off the magnetic axis is found to have a significantly stronger effect
  on the stability of the modes than the value on the magnetic axis.
