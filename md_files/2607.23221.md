abstract: We introduce and simulate an analytically tractable model for an intruder
  of arbitrary shape in a nonequilibrium bath, with chirality originating from the
  bath, the intruder, or their coupling. In the dilute regime, a Langevin description
  derived from a Boltzmann-Lorentz equation shows how intruder geometry governs ratchet
  effects and odd response. In the dense regime, the dynamics of the intruder are
  instead governed by the hydrodynamic modes of the bath and edge currents, which
  are described by a Stokes equation including a chiral torque density. Our results
  link shape to chiral transport and show that odd response arises from distinct mechanisms
  in the dilute and dense limits.
