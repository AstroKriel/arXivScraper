abstract: We introduce a geometric framework that organizes the second-order statistics
  of the Elasser fields into polarization states on generalized Poincaré spheres.
  In this representation, energy, cross-helicity, residual energy, and the phase lag
  between counter-propagating wave packets emerge as complementary polarization parameters.
  We derive a Bloch-analogue equation governing the evolution of polarization states
  and show that distinct polarization geometries are associated with different cascade
  dynamics. The framework predicts that transitions in the turbulent spectral scaling
  coincide with changes in the polarization state of the interacting modes.
