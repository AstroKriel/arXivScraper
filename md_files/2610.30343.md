abstract: We present a novel mechanism for spontaneous shear-flow generation in the
  scrape-off layer (SOL) of magnetically confined plasmas, arising at abrupt changes
  in the sheath connection length $L_\parallel$ . Such transitions occur naturally
  in the Wendelstein 7-X stellarator. Applying a critical balance treatment for interchange
  turbulence, we find that $L_\parallel$ sets the equilibrium pressure gradient and
  hence the radial electric field away from the transition, producing an $E\times
  B$ shear around the transition comparable to the turbulence growth rate. High-fidelity
  simulations are shown to be consistent with the theoretical prediction.
