abstract: Driven net- and no-net-flux MHD turbulence simulations up to $10,\!368^3$
  reveal sign-mixed velocity--magnetic, velocity--vorticity, and magnetic--current
  aligned patches below the energy equipartition scale. The first two angles scale
  as $λ^{1/8}$ and $λ^{1/16}$ , while the third angle varies weakly with scale. We
  develop and test a constant-flux transport model for departures from relaxed states,
  which predicts both exponents. These findings affect eddy anisotropy, reconnection-mediated
  turbulence onset, large-scale dynamos, and the nature of magnetized turbulence.
