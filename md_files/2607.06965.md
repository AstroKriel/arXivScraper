abstract: Magnetic fields generated by the Rayleigh-Taylor instability were measured
  in laser-accelerated planar foils using ultrafast proton radiography. Thin plastic
  foils were irradiated with $\sim$ 4-kJ, 2.5-ns laser pulses focused to an intensity
  of $\sim$ 10 $^{14}$ W $/$ cm $^{2}$ on the OMEGA EP Laser System. Target modulations
  were seeded by laser nonuniformities and amplified during target acceleration by
  the Rayleigh-Taylor instability. The experimental data show the hydrodynamic evolution
  of the target and MG-level magnetic fields generated in the broken foil. The experimental
  data are in good agreement with predictions from 2-D magnetohydrodynamic simulations.
