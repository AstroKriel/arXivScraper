abstract: Cross helicity, the velocity--magnetic-field correlation, is an ideal invariant
  of incompressible magnetohydrodynamics and constrains energy cascades and magnetic-field
  generation. Although cross helicity typically decays via visco-resistive effects,
  we analytically demonstrate that it grows exponentially in sheared inhomogeneous
  turbulence. This cross-helical shear instability is confirmed using three-dimensional
  numerical simulations. Growth requires a nonzero gradient of the Reynolds--Maxwell
  stress to produce turbulent cross helicity; this production overcomes effective
  losses when shear exceeds a threshold set by the ratio of turbulent magnetic to
  momentum diffusivities.
