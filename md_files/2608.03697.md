abstract: Two-dimensional fluids conserve energy and enstrophy, driving inverse energy
  cascades via Fjørtofts argument. We show General Relativity admits an analogous
  structure... for linear radiative perturbations of Petrov type D backgrounds (Kerr,
  Kerr--AdS), the gravitational-wave energy $W = \sum_k W_k$ and magnetic Weyl enstrophy
  $\mathcal{Z} = \int B_{ab} B^{ab} \sqrtγ \, d^3x \approx \sum_k ω_k^2 W_k$ are approximately
  conserved in the zero-angular momentum frame, where vorticity coupling vanishes
  identically and curl exchange cancels mode-by-mode. This yields a gravitational
  Fjørtoft constraint implying nonlinear energy transfer proceeds preferentially toward
  lower frequencies. The constraint is dynamically active in near-extremal Kerr (
  $τ_{\text{damp}} \gg τ_{\text{nl}}$ ) and confined geometries (AdS), but suppressed
  in generic ringdown. In AdS, $\mathcal{Z}$ maps holographically to the boundary
  fluid enstrophy.
