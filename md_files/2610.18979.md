abstract: Charged dust in magnetized gas is subject to aerodynamic drag and the Lorentz
  force, both of which can be difficult to model numerically in a fluid simulation
  where their characteristic timescales are shorter than the timestep set by the Courant
  condition. We present a two-stage general implicit Runge-Kutta (GIRK) source integrator
  for multifluid charged dust coupled to an ideal magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) gas, implemented
  in the open source GPU-accelerated quokka code. Drag and Lorentz forces are advanced
  together in one implicit source update, avoiding errors in the terminal drift that
  can arise when the two forces are advanced in separate substeps. The timestep is
  compared with a drag-Lorentz timescale to select between resolved and stiff branches.
  The resolved branch uses Gauss-Legendre coefficients, while the stiff branch uses
  a coefficient set designed to recover the terminal drift set by the drag-Lorentz
  balance. We also allow the dust stopping time and charge-to-mass ratio to depend
  on the evolving state, evaluating them separately at the two implicit stage states
  and solving the resulting nonlinear stage equations by Picard iteration. The source
  update conserves total gas-dust momentum, and its computational cost scales linearly
  with the number of dust species. Tests reproduce the expected drag damping and terminal
  drift, confirm the predicted gyromotion amplitude and phase errors, demonstrate
  accurate treatment of state-dependent source coefficients, and recover the expected
  wave, shock, and instability dynamics in dusty MHD flows.
