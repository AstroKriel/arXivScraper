abstract: The first 3D kinetic simulation of a magnetron discharge reproduces the
  measured rotating spoke and reveals that density and current rotate in opposite
  directions... the $m=1$ spoke turns in the $\mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{B}$ direction
  at 90~kHz while $m\simeq16$ electron-cyclotron-drift-type filaments turn at 1.0~MHz,
  with no net propagation along $\mathbf{B}$ . Magnetic drifts exchange far more energy
  than they deposit, yet their spoke-front heating sustains the ionization. Following
  helical paths, the anode-directed current is relayed by de-trapping turbulence at
  the sheath edge (50\%) and by the spoke channel in the bulk (97\%).
